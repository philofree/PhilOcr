# The baptism block — copy into your plan when a name is minted

One block per new name. A plan that mints a name without this block filled
has skipped the check.

---

## Name: `__________`  (the full signifier as it will appear)

**1. Head** (the baptised subject — never a bare mechanism verb):

> head: `__________`   qualifier(s): `__________`

**2. Lexicon check** (tick exactly one):

- [ ] **Imported** — signifier found in `PREDICATES.json` baptisms or the
      family registry under entry `__________`; denotation matches.
- [ ] **Imported under existing signifier** — the candidate was a synonym;
      the referent resolves to entry `__________`; using that signifier
      instead.
- [ ] **Imported from the family owner** — the term is owned by
      `.eukoine/` (identity/schema or the family lexicon); importing its
      form verbatim, not re-coining.
- [ ] **Coined** — referent is genuinely new. Registry entry appended in
      this same pass (entry name: `__________`). Disagreements: `__________`
      / none.

**3. Conservation assertion**

> This head is conserved unchanged across every boundary it crosses in
> this change: `__________` (module→module, code→column, code→JSON key,
> code→flag). No boundary re-baptises it.

---

**Reject conditions** — bare-mechanism-verb or generic-plural head; head
mutation across a boundary; a synonym for a bound referent; a coinage
with no same-pass registry entry; a family term re-coined instead of
imported.
