# `/doc-cold-reading` — trace the route before cutting it

Documents liquefy when a changed direction is added beside the old one. A link
graph does not cure that if readers do not follow the links or meet substantive
paraphrase before reaching the owner.

This skill owns semantic documentary appraisal. Epitomikon supplies located,
deterministic observations; the reader decides what they mean. Neither result
authorizes an edit.

## Method

1. **Pin and freeze.** Record repository revision and status. Run Epitomikon
   before semantic reading. Use `--legacy` for an unadopted repository or the
   tracked/explicit contract for an adopted one. Preserve the JSON artifact
   outside the target repository.
2. **Ask blind questions.** Give the reader the repository and concrete
   questions, never the Epitomikon candidate list or an authority briefing.
   Minimum set: purpose, current authority, next work, one changed direction,
   and one historical fact.
3. **Record the route.** For each question record answer, authority cited,
   documents and lines read, citation hops followed, search-discovered files,
   and refusals. Search discovery is not citation following.
4. **Reveal observations.** Only after the trace, compare the reader route with
   exact observation and documentary spans. A duplicate phrase, date, link or
   missing carrier is evidence—not a semantic verdict.
5. **Test healthy twins.** Before proposing a cut, test necessary local warning,
   generated projection, legitimate chronicle, versioned guidance, quotation,
   translation, example, accessibility carrier and necessary product context.
6. **Write the cut brief.** Each item is `integrate`, `retire`, `rewrite`,
   `preserve-as-chronicle`, or `refuse`, and names the current owner, affected
   spans, reader question, expected post-cut route and falsifier.
7. **Confirm after an owner-approved cut.** Re-run the same questions blind.
   Critical answer correctness and authority citation must not decline.

## Reader count

- Ordinary appraisal: one independent reader.
- Calibration or disputed high-impact cut: three independent readers; two must
  agree on the deterministic fact before it is used to calibrate a probe.

Readers do not see each other's work. The coordinator may not act as both the
only tracer and the judge.

## Prospective contract rule

After the repository's adoption revision:

- a new operational Markdown document uses the documentary contract;
- changing an older operational document ratchets the whole file into it;
- an untouched older document remains legacy and is not a defect by age alone;
- a cleanup campaign opens only for an accepted finding regulating one
  documentary variable.

The contract and carrier rules live in `docs/DOCUMENT_CONTRACT.md` in a seeded
repository. Cite that owner; do not copy its field definitions into this skill.

## Refuse the cut when

- removal may reduce answer correctness, authority recovery, accessibility or
  necessary local context;
- current and historical roles cannot be separated at the affected span;
- the supposed duplicate has a distinct referent;
- the reader reached the answer only by search and the intended citation route
  remains untested;
- the repository owner has not authorized documentary changes.

## Disease register (signature questions)

| Disease | Signature question |
|---|---|
| **Restatement drift** | How many documents state this rule? |
| **Displaced authority** | Who may state this rule? |
| **Relay document / section** | What orientation job does this do that the next hop does not? |
| **Purpose-orphan** | Which PURPOSE.md requirement does this section serve? |
| **Doc-rot** | Does the declared authority match the discovered statement distribution? |
| **Grain Collapse / Grain Preservation** | Which span merges unlike particulars or restates lexicon law at a destructive grain? (cite `grain_collapse`; do not restate) |

Fleet governing-document sweep: [`/gov-sweep`](../gov-sweep/SKILL.md).

## Output

Return the route table, assessments and cut brief in the active plan or campaign.
Ordinary appraisal creates no checked-in report and no automatic findings queue.
